Orientation

Where you are

An endless office maze under buzzing fluorescent light — damp yellow wallpaper, mottled carpet, and a hum that never stops. It generates forever in every direction and it does not care that you are here.

The only way is down. Four floors lie beneath you, each stranger and less safe than the last, and somewhere in the wallpaper are the thin places you can slip through. Find them, descend, and try to keep your body — and your mind — intact.

This is not a race. It is a place. Learn its rules or become part of it.

Survival · Body & mind

Staying alive, staying sane

❤ Hit points

The things down here hurt you on contact. Take a hit and the screen jolts red. Level 0 is safe — nothing hunts the lobby — but every floor below has teeth.

Health regrows slowly once you're clear of danger. A bandage patches you fast. Reach zero and you black out, then wake where you fell in.

Listen: a heartbeat rises and quickens as something closes in. If you can hear your pulse, it's already near.

🧠 Sanity

The dark and the hunt eat at your mind. Let it run low and the walls close in violet-black and the whispers start.

Keep it up: your flashlight, a sip of almond water, and — best of all — staying near a friend. You steady each other. Split up and you both fray.

Survival · Light

Light is a resource

Press L for your flashlight — it throws a warm cone that steadies your nerves. Turn it off and the deep floors swallow you (sometimes worth it, to not be seen). A cracked glowstick washes the world green and shoves the fog back for a while. The pipes and the electrical station are black; bring your own light.

The world · Four floors down

The descent

The maze is no longer one endless yellow floor. It is a stack, and each level has its own look, its own things, and its own way down. Your compass (top-left) always points to the nearest exit; the exits themselves are glowing beacons — a beam and a breathing ring. Walk into one and press F to fall through.

The world · Field bestiary

What lives down here

Different floors, different things. Learn their silhouettes in the fog — the shape tells you what it wants.

The world · Company

The lost souls

Not everything here wants to hurt you. Rare, pale figures drift the halls — people who fell through and never found the way up.

Walk up to one and press E. They'll speak: lore, warnings, and — if you listen — the occasional real hint about the way down.

“if the lights go out, stop moving. it hunts movement.”

Supplies · What you'll find

Things left lying around

The Backrooms restocks itself. Walk close to an item and press F to take it; Q uses the selected one; X drops it. Six slots — choose what's worth carrying.

Together · Online co-op

Play with someone

Get into the same world

Type your name on the title screen, hit PLAY ONLINE, pick a room code, and share it. Anyone who enters the same code falls into the same maze — anywhere on the internet, no host and no setup. The first one in decides the world; everyone else inherits it.

See each other

Fellow players appear as pale figures with nameplates in the fog — and a little health bar under each name so you know when to help.

Chat

Press Enter to talk. Messages float as speech bubbles over your head, you'll see when someone's typing…, and /me emotes.

Stay close

A friend nearby steadies your sanity. The halls are less maddening together — so don't wander off alone into the dark.

Meta · Keeping your run

Save, settings & anchors

Continue where you left off

Solo runs auto-save — your level, position, health and whole inventory — every few seconds and on every descent. CONTINUE on the title screen drops you right back in.

Make it yours

The gear ⚙ opens a control panel: music & volume, ambience, film grain, particles, crosshair, head-bob, mouse sensitivity — and, if you want it gentler, turn creatures off or switch to peaceful (no damage).

Anchors — fall through a real place

On the title screen you can paste a Google Maps link (or bare lat,lng) into the anchor field. You'll fall through that spot — and the same place always makes the same maze, for everyone. Settings can even reopen the map where you fell in.

Anchored worlds hold their shape. Unanchored ones forget you were ever there.
