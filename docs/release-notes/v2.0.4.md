## the backrooms 2.0.4 — the maze has a pulse

the halls stayed quiet until something was already on you, and the compass walked you past everything. now you can hear the building and what is in it, and the way down is something you find.

### what changed for players

- **you can hear yourself.** your own steps, different on every floor and harder at a run; a tick when you pick something up or draw from a machine, paper when you read or open the map, a thud as each light goes out down a hall. the footsteps that keep your pace keep it for real now. sweet almond water softens your steps in your own ears too, as much as it does for the things. all of it sits under **ambience**, and none of it is on the block.
- **the compass earns its pull.** a way you have seen still gets an arrow and a distance. until then the compass keeps quiet for your first minute on a floor, or until the floor gives you a hint — a page, a soul's word, a floor plan — and then *something pulls, faintly*: no distance, a little off true, never toward a stairwell up. it used to walk you across a floor in a quarter of a minute; now a floor gets a minute or more of you. the block's compass is as it was.
- **the first minute, written.** on a solo run without a pin, a note near where you arrive in the lobby sends you to a lost soul, and the soul sends you on; the faint pull and the lobby's hint wait for that soul, or for two minutes. the presence there keeps quiet a little while at first, any presence keeps quiet while something hunts you, and with an unread page in reach, **e** reads it first.
- **fewer words.** a floor you reach for the first time says its own line — the deeper floors' lines have never been on screen before — and one more at most while you arrive; after that the building speaks unasked only every so often. the lines that matter still come, and soon: a few breaths after your first way down the file says how it has you, a floor you come back to by a hole, a stairwell or the lift says so (climbing out, the lobby says so in its own way). when you wake from a death, where you woke comes a few seconds in (after the floor's own line, on a floor new to you), and on a floor you know, what your hands let go comes next (or the file's word on your death, if they held nothing); the rest waits until the floor has settled, and goes unsaid if you take a way first. *you rummage.* is gone: the hint reads *searching the cabinet*. below the block, only your first press of the light says anything (going dark in a friend's light still names them), and the smiler's warning comes once; a run you continue hears each once more. murmurs come twelve to a floor, none again before the rest: a few lines in a hundred are repeats, where nearly half were. *there is no exit. keep looking.* is said only when you are truly lost on the lobby, and the lobby's own line no longer says *no exit.*
- **events you hear.** a phone a few rooms over, a chair pushed back next door (the lobby and the habitable zone), a knock along the pipe (pipe dreams), a breaker tripping (the electrical station). more often than not, the first event on a floor you have just reached comes within its first minute, and is heard, not read.
- **drawers that pay.** a find in one drawer in eleven (it was sixteen), an empty one in three (it was half), and twice as much to read. some hold a floor plan that marks one thing on your map. a searched drawer says *searched.*; one you cannot search yet, *not now. something is close.* the things hear the runners squeal.
- **you hear them before you see them.** a thing near you through the halls that has not found you yet makes its own sound now and then, from where it is, and close by a slow heartbeat starts before anything hunts you, and stops when the thing goes — never through a wall (badly hurt, you hear it anyway). in the test walks, four sightings in five on levels one and two came with that warning. seen from off your screen, the line is *something has seen you.*, and its sound comes from where it is.
- **the things move house.** the watcher wanders pipe dreams now, beside the crawler. and the creatures of levels one to three no longer stand in the same rooms on every floor and in every world: each floor, each pin and each room lays them out its own way, the same for everyone who shares it.
- **small things.** the machine hands you *a bottle of almond water*, and everything else with its own article.

### update together

in a room, everyone should be on 2.0.4: a 2.0.3 copy and a 2.0.4 copy in the same room place the things on levels one to three differently, so you will not agree on where they stand. nothing crashes, every shared verb works as before, and nothing new goes over the wire.

a solo run saved on 2.0.3 continues where you left it, but the first time you continue one on levels one to three, the things there stand somewhere new, and one you had driven off may be back sooner, from somewhere else. it happens once.

### install, on every system

- **macos** `The-Backrooms-2.0.4-universal.dmg` (or `.zip`), one build for intel and apple silicon
  - macos 13 (ventura) or later. on macos 12, keep 2.0.2, or play in the browser
  - not notarized yet: the first time, right-click the app → **open** (or `xattr -cr "/Applications/The Backrooms.app"`)
- **windows** `The-Backrooms-Setup-2.0.4.exe` · **linux** `The-Backrooms-2.0.4-x86_64.AppImage` (updates itself) or `The-Backrooms-2.0.4-amd64.deb`
- windows and appimage installs on 2.0.x move to 2.0.4 on their own, or when you ask, whichever you chose in settings
- the browser copy picks it up the next time you open it (sometimes the time after)
