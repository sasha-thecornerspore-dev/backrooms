## the backrooms 2.0.0 — the building has weight now

the biggest update yet. the maze is solid, the things in it hunt, the floors connect, and who you are depends on how you came in.

### install, on every system

- **windows** `The-Backrooms-Setup-2.0.0.exe` · **linux** `.AppImage` (updates itself) or `.deb` · **macos** one universal `.dmg` for intel and apple silicon
- macos builds are not notarized yet: the first time, right-click the app → **open**
- existing windows installs update to 2.0.0 on their own

### updates, your way

settings ⚙ → **updates**: **automatic** (downloads quietly, installs when you quit), **ask before downloading**, or **only when i check**. there is a **check for updates** button in every mode. automatic no longer restarts the game mid-run.

### things are solid

- furniture, machines, landmarks and the lost souls are bodies: you stop against them and slide along them
- rooms are furnished like rooms: cabinets along real walls, chairs round a box, drums in the pump room, panels in the switch room
- no corridor is ever sealed — every placement is checked to keep the maze walkable
- don't want it? settings ⚙ → **solid furniture**

### the things hunt

- they **hear**: a sprint carries round corners, a walk barely does, and sound follows the halls, not straight through walls
- they **see** only with a clear line of sight
- they go alert, investigate the noise, hunt you, then search where they lost you — and they go *around* the cabinet you are hiding behind
- each kind has one rule you can learn: the smiler only moves when you are not looking; the hound gathers itself, then lunges; the lurker moves only in the dark; the crawler takes your ankles when the light leaves it; the tesla arcs at range and goes for a radio left talking; the watcher costs you to look at

### the fight

- **space** tap to ward, **hold** to charge a longer, harder push
- a ward landed in the moment a reeling thing turns back to face you counts double
- **x** sets a thing down — a radio left talking draws them; a glowstick marks the way
- the polaroid flash blinds; sweet almond water makes you quiet; bandaging mid-chase is a committed second
- being hunted thickens the music and brings your heart into your ears — no new hud

### the floors connect

- stairwells **up**, paired with the holes you fell through, and a one-way lift on level 1
- floors remember what you took, where you stood, and which machines you emptied; they restock only while you are away
- **tab** opens a pencil map of only what you have walked and seen, per floor, held while you keep moving
- the compass points at ways you have actually seen, and back the way you came
- dying wakes you a floor above, beside the hole — lighter

### between encounters

- search cabinets, crates and drums (**f**): mostly words, sometimes a find
- some places are wrong every time: a figure standing where you just were, chairs that have been moved, water that keeps pace

### who you are — the form on the counter

<!-- finalised after the faction review -->
the file writes you into a column the first time you go through a way — from how you came in, never chosen:

- **the tenant** — the file has you at an address. the street is not there.
- **the anchored** — your body is still at the pin. the farther you walk from it, the more the dark pulls.
- **the unnamed** — the file cannot spell you. the things lose you faster, and the ending is closed until you sign.
- **the processed** — the file opened a line on you, and you can read its format.
- **minted thin** — not all of you arrived. the things see you only when you move.

and what you want stamped on your file — **extension**, **compliance** or **litigation** — each with its own way out.

### together

- **c** whistles: friends hear it from where you stand, and it keeps a roll call
- with a friend on your floor, a fatal hit lays you **down** for 25 s — they kneel beside you, light on, and count you back
- a friend's light shelters you; a set-down thing can carry a note and an arrow for whoever finds it
