## the backrooms 2.0.7 — the crew

a room was not a crew. when its line dropped you were left alone in the maze without a word, and nobody could see who was where, or what file a friend was under. now the game says when the line goes dead and calls it back, `/who` says who is in the room in words, the room hears when a friend's file moves, a friend's whistle pencils itself on your map, and the film names the word a friend is filed under. the body no longer gives its place in numbers. and the relay gets limits on how fast one player can send.

### what changed for players

- **the dropped line is said, and called back.** when a room's line drops — a network that blinks, a relay update — the game says *the line went dead. the others are gone.* and keeps a plate in the chat until it is back. meanwhile it asks for the same room again, under the same name, a little longer between each try: *still trying the line.* after four minutes it stops: *the line stays dead. nobody is trying it now.* press **r** to try at once — the plate's last row says so, *r · call the line back* — or, on a phone, tap the plate: *tap · call the line back*. it still works after the tries have stopped. when the room takes you back: *the line holds again.* coming back is not walking in: the file keeps how you first arrived, and a cache you set down is not laid down twice. while the line is down nothing reaches the others: a cache you take is told to them once you are back, a word you would leave stays in your hands (*your hands are not ready.*), and a chat line goes nowhere (*no one is here to hear it.*). none of it is said on the block, where no room goes.
- **a friend's line coming back reads as one line.** a friend who drops and comes back under the same name (any but the blank `wanderer` that many share) within a minute and a half reads as *— maddie's line came back.*, in place of their leaving and their arriving. so does a friend who was in the room when your own line dropped, coming in after you.
- **a room that cuts you off for flooding says so.** the plate says only *the line was cut. too much went down it, too fast. leave it be a while.* there is no press and no try: the game does not call that room again by itself. refused that way as you join, the title says *failed: too fast*. a 2.0.7 copy holds back where it stands while its line is silent, so an honest one does not meet it (the relay guard, below).
- **`/who`.** press enter and type `/who` (on a phone, **TYPE** first): the room in words. you first, then each friend, with the floor by its name and the word the file has them under: *you · pipe dreams · filed under extension.* · *maddie · the lobby · filed under litigation.* the block is never part of a room, and a friend there would show no floor: *ada · filed under compliance.* a friend whose game has not said lately where it is: *bo · the file has not placed them.* alone: *no one is here but you.* on the block, `/who` answers with a line the field terminal already has: *the file holds nothing from here.* the console's help line names it now: *the file does not recognise that. try /recover, /cases, /file &lt;answer&gt;, /intake, /status, /who or /invite.* (on the block it reads as it did before).
- **a friend re-filed.** when a friend's file moves to a new word, the room says so: *— maddie re-filed under litigation.* not the word they came in under, only a change; never about you, never on the block.
- **a friend from your square.** once a session, a friend whose pin came from the same square as yours is said as they come in: *— maddie came in from your square.* the room's own square counts too. it says no more than the film already does — *the film develops maddie. the film shows your pin.* — and nothing is said if either of you came without a pin.
- **the film names a friend's word.** a photograph of a friend now ends with the word their file is under: *the film develops maddie. there is a stamp on them. filed under litigation.* a friend who is down too. never on the block.
- **a friend's whistle on your map.** on your floor, a friend's whistle leaves a faint pencil mark (∿) on your map where they stood. it fades over about a minute, one mark for each friend, and the sheet never keeps it. your own whistle leaves none; the block has no map.
- **the body in words.** with a pin, the building used to say where your body is in numbers, close enough to find the door on any stream or screenshot. now it says *your body remains where you fell through.* the film's anchored pin says *the film shows where you fell through. it is 40 m behind you and it has not moved.* (the distance as before, no place), the form says *address: where you fell through. the body is there.*, and the title screen, once a pin is read, *anchored — you will fall through that place.*
- **a joiner who brings a pin is told first.** until now only whoever opened a room was told what it learns of a pin. now, with a pin in the field, an invite's plate, **JOIN LAN** and the PLAY ONLINE box say it before you go in: *a room you join with this pin hands the others a mark of its neighbourhood — with marks from two rooms, or a guess at your city, enough to find which square kilometre. to someone who already has a few of your addresses, that is the door.* in the PLAY ONLINE box it follows the opener's warning, since a code may already have a room behind it.

every new line, as the game says it: *the line went dead. the others are gone.* · *still trying the line.* · *the line stays dead. nobody is trying it now.* · *the line holds again.* · *the line was cut. too much went down it, too fast. leave it be a while.* · *r · call the line back* · *tap · call the line back* · *failed: too fast* · *— maddie's line came back.* · *no one is here but you.* · *you · pipe dreams · filed under extension.* · *ada · filed under compliance.* · *bo · the file has not placed them.* · *— maddie re-filed under litigation.* · *— maddie came in from your square.* · *the film develops maddie. there is a stamp on them. filed under litigation.* · *your body remains where you fell through.* · *the film shows where you fell through. it is 40 m behind you and it has not moved.* · *address: where you fell through. the body is there.* · *anchored — you will fall through that place.* · *a room you join with this pin hands the others a mark of its neighbourhood — with marks from two rooms, or a guess at your city, enough to find which square kilometre. to someone who already has a few of your addresses, that is the door.*

### update together

nothing new goes over the wire between players, and a room can mix 2.0.6 and 2.0.7. everything above is read off what a room already carried: the players list, each friend's heartbeat (the same version, the same fields), the whistle. so a 2.0.7 player sees a 2.0.6 friend in `/who`, hears their file move, sees their whistle on the map and their word on the film. a 2.0.6 player sees none of it: no `/who`, no re-filed or square lines, the film and the map as before. on a 2.0.6 screen, a 2.0.7 friend whose line drops and comes back reads as a leaving and an arrival, as it always did. a 2.0.6 copy whose own line drops is still left in an empty maze without a word, until its player joins again by hand. both send where they stand at the same rate.

a solo run saved on 2.0.6 continues where you left it: nothing in the save changed, and a whistle mark is never part of it.

### the relay guard

players see nothing of it. until now a room took movement, chat and typing as fast as anyone sent them. now each has a limit, twice the most an honest copy sends, and a frame over it is dropped without a word. a 2.0.7 copy whose line stalls holds back where it stands until the room answers again, so what it queued does not arrive all at once. a socket that keeps flooding is seen out of the room and closed with code 4029, reason `too fast`: the one new signal of this release, and the one close a 2.0.7 copy takes as do not call again. the lan server in the desktop app keeps the same limits. and the atlas's check-ins and drop-ins now take from one guard that every sender shares, never one per visitor, so a flood can no longer run up the cost, though while one lasts the doors refuse everyone. the guard keeps no address.

the relay guard goes out on its own deploy, at an hour the owner picks. a relay deploy drops every room: a 2.0.7 copy says the line went dead and comes back by itself, while a 2.0.6 copy that drops does not rejoin by itself — its player is left in an empty maze until they join again. so it goes out once most players have 2.0.7, at a quiet hour.

### privacy

- nothing new goes over the wire between players. the one new signal is the relay's close, sent only to the socket it closes.
- the body line no longer shows coordinates. neither do the film's anchored pin, the form's address or the title's anchored line. the title's anchor field still shows what you pasted into it.
- `/who`, the re-filed line and the square line are read off what the room already had; the square line says no more than the film's *the film shows your pin.* nothing on the block is new.
- the game never asks for your location.
- the atlas's shared guard keeps only the times of check-ins and drop-ins: no address, and no hash of one.

### for whoever runs the relay and the site

- **the relay guard deploys with this push:** `Relay (Cloudflare Worker)` deploys on a push to main that touches `relay/`, a game file the relay imports (`src/renderer/anchor.js`, `origin-intake.js`, `status.js`, `prefs.js`, `scraps.js`, `src/wish-labels.js`) or the workflow itself, and this release touches `relay/` and `origin-intake.js`. so the push that brings 2.0.7 to main is the relay deploy, unless the `relay` environment holds it: choose that hour, or give the environment a required reviewer and hold the deploy job until the hour you choose. the limits, the 4029 close and the shared atlas guard are in `docs/operations.md` §2.
- **the movement rate:** `world.json` now ships `"net": { "posHz": 20 }`, how many times a second a player in a room sends where they stand. it stays at 20. lowered to 10 it halves those frames, which the relay's free plan meters (incoming websocket messages, twenty to a billed request); the browser copy follows on its next site deploy, the desktop app with its next release. `node tools/validate-world.mjs` refuses anything but a whole number from 5 to 20.
- **the site's pages are copied by hand:** when this goes out, put `docs/manual.html` at `manual/index.html` on gh-pages. it now covers the dropped line and **r**, `/who`, the re-filed and square lines, the film's word, the whistle's mark on the map, the body in words and the joiner's warning.
