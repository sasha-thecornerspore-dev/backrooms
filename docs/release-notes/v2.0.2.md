## the backrooms 2.0.2 — rooms share the neighbourhood, never the pin

a pin used to go into a room whole. on 2.0.1 and every copy before it, a room opened with a pin was drawn from that exact pin, and the relay keeps a room's world for good and hands it to everyone who enters the code: anyone holding the code could read the spot back out of it, to about eleven metres. the mark a joiner sent was nearly as fine — with a guess at the city, marks from two rooms found the door — and an invite link with your place in it carried the pin to four decimals. solo never shared it. 2.0.2 stops the rest.

### what changed

- **a room is told only your pin's neighbourhood**: the pin rounded to two decimals, a cell about 1.1 km north to south and 0.86 km east to west at baltimore's latitude. online or lan, host or joiner, a room never gets the pin itself
- **open a room with a pin** and its world is drawn from that neighbourhood, not from your door — the same halls for anyone who opens it from that square kilometre
- **the host is told.** the PLAY ONLINE box says so before you press ENTER, and the chat says it once more as you arrive: *this room is drawn from your pin's neighbourhood. anyone with the code can see which square kilometre — to someone who already has a few of your addresses, that is the door.* a square kilometre picks a door out of a short list. if anyone with the code might already have a few addresses for you, play that room without a pin
- **join a room someone else drew** and the others get only a mark of your neighbourhood, never finer, however many rooms you join. the chat says that too
- **an invite link** with your place in it carries the same two decimals, and no more
- **solo is unchanged**: the same pin gives the same halls it always did. a solo run sends the pin nowhere unless you ask — a phone's short link is read once at the relay, **locate your body** opens the map, and a beacon you aim at your own address carries it there

### rooms opened before

a room opened with a pin on 2.0.1 or earlier still holds that exact pin's world on the relay. a room's world always wins over whoever comes later, so a newer copy cannot take it back: leave that code and open a new one. a 2.0.2 copy that finds itself in a room drawn from its own pin says so: *this room was drawn from your pin itself, by an older copy. anyone with the code can find the door. leave the code and open a new one.*

an older copy still sends its exact pin's seed whenever it opens or joins a room, and its finer mark. update before you bring a pin into a room, and ask whoever you play with to update too. walking, chat and everything else work across versions as before.

### install, on every system

- **macos** `The-Backrooms-2.0.2-universal.dmg` (or `.zip`), one build for intel and apple silicon
  - not notarized yet: the first time, right-click the app → **open** (or `xattr -cr "/Applications/The Backrooms.app"`)
- **windows** `The-Backrooms-Setup-2.0.2.exe` · **linux** `The-Backrooms-2.0.2-x86_64.AppImage` (updates itself) or `The-Backrooms-2.0.2-amd64.deb`
- windows and appimage installs on 2.0.1 or 2.0.0 move to 2.0.2 on their own, or when you ask, whichever you chose in settings
- the browser copy picks it up the next time you open it (sometimes the time after)
