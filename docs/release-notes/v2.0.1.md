## the backrooms 2.0.1 — the mac door opens

2.0.0 went out without its mac installer: the upload step stumbled on the older shell macOS ships with, and the `.dmg` never reached the release. this one carries it. the game itself is the same 2.0.0 building, unchanged: everything in [the 2.0.0 notes](https://github.com/sasha-thecornerspore-dev/backrooms/releases/tag/v2.0.0) still holds.

### install, on every system

- **macos** `The-Backrooms-2.0.1-universal.dmg` (or `.zip`), one build for intel and apple silicon
  - not notarized yet: the first time, right-click the app → **open** (or `xattr -cr "/Applications/The Backrooms.app"`)
- **windows** `The-Backrooms-Setup-2.0.1.exe` · **linux** `.AppImage` (updates itself) or `.deb`
- windows and appimage installs on 2.0.0 move to 2.0.1 on their own, or when you ask, whichever you chose in settings
