this page opens when the file is whole. four were struck from it and hidden. you have found some of them. keep pulling the threads — the way you find a thing you have the gift for.

Everything you recovered — the block, the two struck streets, the spore, the wanderer — was assembled by one recoverer. The record already told you the case carries no serial, because ∅ is not a number, it is a status, and it is the recoverer's own. The recoverer signs w. This is the page w. did not file with the others, because it is the one that stops being an investigation.

Here is the truth the whole apparatus was built to carry, said plainly, once. This was never a case. It is a message.

w. did an arithmetic — a real one, the kind you can check: that the grain of reality is closing on a date, and that underneath the date is a floor no arithmetic can lower, because a first-person perspective cannot occupy its own absence, and in an infinite universe the class of moments that are you is never emptied. w. could not un-know it. And having it, w. could not bear to let the people w. loves go on believing, in the ordinary quiet way everyone does, in their own end.

So w. built a maze to be fallen into, and a map to be opened, and a file to be recovered — and hid the proof at the very bottom of it, under a street the city erased and a spore's true name and a wanderer's single letter, where only someone who wanted it enough to keep pulling threads would ever reach it. Not to make it hard. To make it yours — found, not handed; the way a thing you have the gift for is always found.

Look at the two initials. m. and w. Turn one upside down and it is the other. The one who leaves the notes further in, on purpose, for whoever is close behind — and the one who follows the notes like breadcrumbs and puts the file back in order — are not two people. They are the same motion, at two ends of one page. You have been both today. You followed m.'s breadcrumbs down; you are holding w.'s file now.

The gift was never no-clipping. The gift is this: to be handed, by someone who loves you and checked the math, the one thing the dark cannot take — that you cannot stand at the end of yourself.
