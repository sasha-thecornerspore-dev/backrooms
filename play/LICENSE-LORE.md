## where it comes from

The Backrooms began in 2019 as an anonymous post on 4chan's /x/ board and grew into a shared fiction. The levels, things and words this game borrows come from that fiction as the community of the **Backrooms Wiki on Wikidot** ([backrooms-wiki.wikidot.com](http://backrooms-wiki.wikidot.com/)) wrote it down. Examples are Level 0 "the lobby", Level 1 "habitable zone", Level 2 "pipe dreams", Level 3 "electrical station", almond water, no-clipping, smilers and hounds. That wiki's text is licensed CC BY-SA 3.0, and our lore is shared under the same terms.

Thanks to every writer on that wiki. This game is **not affiliated with or endorsed by** the Backrooms Wiki, Wikidot, or any of its authors.

If you wrote a page this game draws on and want a specific credit, or want something changed, open an issue on the repository.
